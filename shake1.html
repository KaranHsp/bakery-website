Berry Milkshake

Item Name

Berry Milkshake (vanilla ice cream blended with mixed berries, finished with berry sauce, whipped cream, cookie crumbs and wafer rolls)

How It Is Made

  1. Blend the vanilla ice cream, milk and frozen mixed berries until thick and smooth.
  2. Drizzle a spoonful of berry sauce down the inside of each cup.
  3. Pour the milkshake into the cups over the sauce.
  4. Pipe whipped cream on top, then sprinkle with cookie crumbs and sprinkles.
  5. Add a little more berry sauce over the cream and slide in the wafer rolls.
  6. Serve immediately with straws.

Serving

Makes 2 shakes

Chef

Chef Harpreet Singh

Ingredients

  • Vanilla ice cream - 2 cups
  • Milk - 0.5 cup
  • Frozen mixed berries - 1 cup
  • Berry sauce - 3 tbsp
  • Whipped cream - 0.5 cup
  • Cookie crumbs - 2 tbsp
  • Sprinkles - 1 tsp
  • Wafer rolls - 2

Team Size

2 members (including the chef)

Prep Time

15 minutes

Serving Guide

  • One shake per guest
  • Serve straight away while cold
  • Wafer rolls are best eaten within 10 minutes

Customisation

  • Vanilla, chocolate or strawberry ice cream base
  • Oreo or biscuit crumbs in place of cookie crumbs
  • No whipped cream on request
