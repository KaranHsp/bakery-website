Fruit cake

Cake Name

Fresh Fruit Tart with Vanilla Custard (9 inch buttery pastry shell, topped with seasonal berries and kiwi)

How It Is Made

  1. Rub cold butter into flour and icing sugar, then add the egg yolk and vanilla to form a dough.
  2. Chill the dough for 30 minutes, then press it into a 9 inch tart pan.
  3. Bake the shell blind until golden, then let it cool completely.
  4. Cook milk, sugar, egg yolks and cornflour into a thick vanilla custard, then stir in the butter and vanilla.
  5. Cool the custard, then spread it evenly inside the shell.
  6. Arrange strawberries, raspberries, blackberries and kiwi in circles over the custard.
  7. Brush the fruit with warm apricot glaze and chill for one hour before serving.

Serving

Serves 10 guests

Chef

Chef Harpreet Singh

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour - 1.5 cups
  • Cold butter - 0.5 cup
  • Icing sugar - 0.25 cup
  • Egg yolk - 1
  • Vanilla extract - 2 tsp
  • Milk - 2 cups
  • Egg yolks (for custard) - 4
  • Sugar - 0.5 cup
  • Cornflour - 3 tbsp
  • Strawberries - 250 g
  • Raspberries - 100 g
  • Blackberries - 100 g
  • Kiwi - 2
  • Apricot glaze - 3 tbsp

Team Size

2 members (including the chef)

Prep Time

1 day

Cutting Guide

  • 9 inch tart: about 10 slices
  • Half tart: about 5 slices
  • Mini tarts: one per guest

Customisation

  • Choice of seasonal fruit
  • Chocolate pastry base
  • Name piped in white chocolate
