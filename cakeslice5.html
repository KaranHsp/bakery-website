Chocolate Drip Cake

Cake Name

Strawberry First Birthday Cake (6 inch, pink strawberry buttercream with fondant strawberries, white flowers and a "ONE" topper)

How It Is Made

  1. Bake two chocolate sponge layers in 6 inch pans for 25 to 30 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.
  2. Cool completely, then level the tops.
  3. Beat butter, icing sugar and cocoa powder until smooth, then add milk to loosen.
  4. Fill the layers with chocolate buttercream and coat the whole cake with a thin crumb layer.
  5. Chill for 20 minutes to firm up.
  6. Warm the ganache slightly, then pour it around the top edge so it runs down the sides in drips.
  7. Chill for 20 minutes, then pipe chocolate buttercream rosettes around the top edge.
  8. Fill the centre with ganache and sprinkle chocolate pearls over it.

Serving

Serves 8 guests

Chef

Chef Harpreet Singh

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour - 1.5 cups
  • Cocoa powder (for sponge) - 0.5 cup
  • Sugar - 1 cup
  • Butter (for sponge) - 0.5 cup
  • Eggs - 2
  • Buttermilk - 0.75 cup
  • Hot coffee or water - 0.5 cup
  • Vanilla extract - 1 tsp
  • Baking soda - 1 tsp
  • Butter (for buttercream) - 1 cup
  • Icing sugar - 3 cups
  • Cocoa powder (for buttercream) - 0.25 cup
  • Dark chocolate (for ganache) - 150 g
  • Cream (for ganache) - 0.5 cup
  • Chocolate pearls - 0.5 cup

Team Size

3 members (including the chef)

Prep Time

2 day

Cutting Guide

  • 6 inch round: about 8 slices
  • Half cake: about 4 slices
  • Cut slices from the centre for clean drips

Customisation

  • White chocolate drips
  • Fresh berries on top
  • Custom name or message in chocolate
