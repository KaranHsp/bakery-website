Chocolate Hazelnut Milkshake

Item Name

Chocolate Hazelnut Milkshake (chocolate ice cream blended with hazelnut spread, topped with whipped cream, chopped hazelnuts and a chocolate truffle)

How It Is Made

  1. Blend the chocolate ice cream, milk, hazelnut spread and chocolate sauce until thick and smooth.
  2. Brush the rim of each glass with chocolate sauce, then dip it into the chopped hazelnuts.
  3. Pour the milkshake into the glasses.
  4. Pipe whipped cream on top in a swirl.
  5. Drizzle with chocolate sauce and add a chocolate hazelnut truffle.
  6. Serve immediately with a long spoon.

Serving

Makes 2 shakes

Chef

Chef Harpreet Singh

Ingredients

  • Chocolate ice cream - 2 cups
  • Milk - 0.5 cup
  • Chocolate hazelnut spread - 3 tbsp
  • Chocolate sauce - 2 tbsp, plus 1 tbsp for drizzle
  • Whipped cream - 0.75 cup
  • Chopped roasted hazelnuts - 0.25 cup
  • Chocolate hazelnut truffles - 2

Team Size

3 members (including the chef)

Prep Time

15 minutes

Serving Guide

  • One shake per guest
  • Serve immediately while thick
  • Provide a long spoon for the cream and truffle

Customisation

  • Caramel sauce in place of chocolate
  • Dark chocolate base
  • Almond or peanut crumble on the rim
