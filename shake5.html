Cookie Milkshake

Item Name

Cookie Milkshake (vanilla ice cream blended with crushed chocolate sandwich cookies, finished with chocolate sauce, whipped cream and a whole cookie)

How It Is Made

  1. Crush 4 of the chocolate sandwich cookies into coarse crumbs and set aside 2 tbsp for garnish.
  2. Blend the vanilla ice cream, milk and the remaining crushed cookies until thick and smooth.
  3. Spoon a little chocolate sauce into the bottom and sides of each glass.
  4. Pour the milkshake into the glasses over the sauce.
  5. Pipe whipped cream on top, then sprinkle with the reserved cookie crumbs.
  6. Press a whole cookie into the cream and add a straw.

Serving

Makes 5 shakes

Chef

Chef Harpreet Singh

Ingredients

  • Vanilla ice cream - 2 cups
  • Milk - 0.5 cup
  • Chocolate sandwich cookies - 6 (4 crushed for the shake, 2 whole for topping)
  • Chocolate sauce - 3 tbsp
  • Whipped cream - 0.75 cup

Team Size

3 members (including the chef)

Prep Time

15 minutes

Serving Guide

  • One shake per guest
  • Serve in a footed glass with a straw
  • Serve immediately while thick

Customisation

  • Vanilla or strawberry ice cream base
  • Extra cookie crumbs in the shake
  • No whipped cream on request
