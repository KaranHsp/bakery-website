Apple Turnover

Item Name

Lattice Apple Turnover (puff pastry filled with spiced apples, finished with a lattice top and apricot glaze)

How It Is Made

  1. Peel and dice the apples, then cook them with butter, brown sugar, cinnamon and lemon juice until soft.
  2. Stir in the cornflour and cook for one more minute until thick. Cool the filling completely.
  3. Roll out the puff pastry and cut it into six rectangles.
  4. Spoon filling down the centre of each rectangle and fold the pastry over to seal the edges.
  5. Cut a lattice of holes across the top layer so the filling shows through.
  6. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 200 C for 20 to 25 minutes, until deep golden.
  7. While still warm, brush the tops with apricot glaze.

Serving

Makes 6 turnovers

Chef

Chef Harpreet Singh

Ingredients

  • Puff pastry - 500 g (2 sheets)
  • Apples - 3 medium
  • Butter - 2 tbsp
  • Brown sugar - 0.25 cup
  • Cinnamon - 1 tsp
  • Lemon juice - 1 tbsp
  • Cornflour - 1 tsp
  • Egg - 1 (for egg wash)

Team Size

2 members (including the chef)

Prep Time

1/2 day

Storage

  • Store in an airtight container at room temperature
  • Reheat for 5 minutes at 160 C to crisp the pastry
  • Best eaten on the day of delivery

Customisation

  • Raspberry or plain glaze
  • Mini size for parties
  • Extra cinnamon in the filling
