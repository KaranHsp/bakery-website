Blueberry Muffin

Item Name

Blueberry Hazelnut Muffin (soft vanilla muffin studded with blueberries, topped with chopped hazelnuts and coarse sugar)

How It Is Made

  1. Preheat the oven to 190 C and line a 12 cup muffin tin with paper cases.
  2. Whisk flour, baking powder and salt together in a large bowl.
  3. In another bowl, whisk sugar, eggs, milk, melted butter and vanilla until smooth.
  4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and mix gently, just until no flour is visible.
  5. Toss the blueberries in a spoon of flour, then fold them into the batter.
  6. Divide the batter between the cases, filling each about three quarters full.
  7. Scatter chopped hazelnuts and coarse sugar on top, then bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
  8. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then move to a rack.

Serving

Makes 12 muffins

Chef

Chef Harpreet Singh

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour - 2 cups
  • Sugar - 0.75 cup
  • Eggs - 2
  • Milk - 1 cup
  • Melted butter - 0.5 cup
  • Vanilla extract - 1 tsp
  • Blueberries - 1.5 cups (fresh or frozen)
  • Chopped hazelnuts - 0.25 cup

Team Size

2 members (including the chef)

Prep Time

1 hour

Serving Guide

  • One muffin per guest
  • Serve at room temperature
  • Pairs well with tea or coffee

Customisation

  • Walnuts or almonds instead of hazelnuts
  • Lemon zest in the batter
  • Mini muffins for parties
