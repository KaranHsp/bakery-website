Classic Vanilla Wedding Cake

Cake Name

Strawberry First Birthday Cake (6 inch, pink strawberry buttercream with fondant strawberries, white flowers and a "ONE" topper)

How It Is Made

  1. Bake two vanilla sponge layers in 6 inch pans for 25 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.
  2. Cool completely, then level the tops.
  3. Beat butter and icing sugar until smooth, then mix in freeze-dried strawberry powder, milk and a drop of pink colouring.
  4. Fill the layers with strawberry buttercream and coat the whole cake with a thin crumb layer.
  5. Chill for 20 minutes, then pipe the final coat and smooth or ruffle the sides.
  6. Pipe a ruffled ring on top and place the fondant strawberries and white flowers evenly around the cake.
  7. Add the "ONE" topper just before delivery.

Serving

Serves 8 guests

Chef

Chef Harpreet Singh

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour - 1.5 cups
  • Sugar - 1 cup
  • Butter (for sponge) - 0.5 cup
  • Eggs - 2
  • Milk (for sponge) - 0.5 cup
  • Vanilla extract - 1 tsp
  • Baking powder - 1 tsp
  • Butter (for buttercream) - 1 cup
  • Icing sugar - 3 cups
  • Freeze-dried strawberry powder - 2 tbsp
  • Pink food colouring - 2 drops
  • Fondant strawberries - 12
  • Fondant white flowers - 5
  • "ONE" wooden topper - 1

Team Size

2 members (including the chef)

Prep Time

1 day

Cutting Guide

  • 6 inch round: about 8 slices
  • Remove the topper before cutting
  • Extra slices: serve with fresh strawberries

Customisation

  • Change the topper to any name or age
  • Different buttercream colours
  • Fresh strawberries on top
