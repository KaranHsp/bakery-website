Chocolate Eclair

Item Name

Chocolate Eclair (crisp choux pastry filled with chocolate pastry cream, topped with dark chocolate glaze and a chocolate disc)

How It Is Made

  1. Heat water, butter, sugar and salt in a pan until the butter melts and the mixture boils.
  2. Take off the heat, add the flour all at once and beat until a smooth dough forms.
  3. Beat in the eggs one at a time until the paste is glossy and holds its shape.
  4. Pipe 10 cm strips onto a lined tray and bake at 200 C for 20 minutes, then at 170 C for 10 to 15 minutes, until hollow and dry.
  5. Cool completely. Whisk milk, egg yolks, sugar and cornflour, then cook until thick.
  6. Take off the heat and stir in the chocolate, butter and vanilla. Chill for 1 hour.
  7. Pipe the chocolate cream into each eclair through a hole in the base.
  8. Melt the glaze chocolate with warm cream, dip the tops, then add a chocolate disc while still soft.

Serving

Makes 8 eclairs

Chef

Chef Harpreet Singh

Ingredients

  • Water - 1 cup
  • Butter - 0.5 cup
  • Sugar - 1 tsp
  • Salt - 0.25 tsp
  • All-purpose flour - 1 cup
  • Eggs - 4
  • Milk (for cream) - 1.5 cups
  • Egg yolks (for cream) - 3
  • Sugar (for cream) - 0.25 cup
  • Cornflour - 2 tbsp
  • Dark chocolate (for cream) - 100 g
  • Butter (for cream) - 1 tbsp
  • Vanilla extract - 1 tsp
  • Dark chocolate (for glaze) - 100 g
  • Cream (for glaze) - 0.25 cup
  • Chocolate discs - 8

Team Size

2 members (including the chef)

Prep Time

1 day

Serving Guide

  • One eclair per guest
  • Serve chilled
  • Take out of the fridge 15 minutes before serving

Customisation

  • Coffee cream filling
  • White chocolate glaze
  • Hazelnut praline topping
