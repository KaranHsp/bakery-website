Our Story

Golden Crumb started with a simple idea: bake every day as if we were baking for our own family. What began as a small kitchen and a handful of regular customers has grown into a neighbourhood bakery known for fresh bread, pastries and celebration cakes.

Every cake, croissant and shake on our menu is made in our own kitchen, in small batches, with real butter and fresh fruit. We take the time to get each recipe right, and we are happy to adapt them for allergies, dietary needs and special occasions.
Our motto is Bakery With Love. It guides how we work as a team, how we treat our customers, and how we look after the place we serve.
