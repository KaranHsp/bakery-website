Strawberry Cream Soda

Item Name

Strawberry Cream Soda (chilled strawberry base topped with sparkling water, whipped cream and strawberry sauce)

How It Is Made

  1. Blend the strawberries, strawberry syrup, lemon juice and a handful of ice until smooth.
  2. Spoon the strawberry base into each glass until it is about half full.
  3. Top up gently with chilled sparkling water, pouring down the side of the glass.
  4. Pipe whipped cream on top.
  5. Drizzle with strawberry sauce and add a straw.

Serving

Makes 6 shakes

Chef

Chef Harpreet Singh

Ingredients

  • Fresh or frozen strawberries - 1 cup
  • Strawberry syrup - 2 tbsp
  • Lemon juice - 1 tsp
  • Ice - 1 cup
  • Chilled sparkling water - 0.75 cup
  • Whipped cream - 0.5 cup
  • Strawberry sauce - 1 tbsp

Team Size

3 members (including the chef)

Prep Time

15 minutes

Serving Guide

  • One drink per guest
  • Serve immediately while fizzy
  • Stir gently before drinking to mix the cream

Customisation

  • Raspberry or mixed berry base
  • Plain soda without cream
  • Extra strawberry sauce on top
