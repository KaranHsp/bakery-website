Butter Croissant

Item Name

Classic Butter Croissant (laminated yeast dough with layers of butter, baked golden and flaky)

How It Is Made

  1. Mix flour, sugar, salt, yeast, milk, water and 2 tbsp soft butter into a smooth dough. Chill for 1 hour.
  2. Shape the cold butter into a flat square between two sheets of parchment.
  3. Roll the dough into a square, place the butter in the centre and fold the dough over it to enclose it.
  4. Roll out the dough and fold it in thirds, then chill for 30 minutes. Repeat the rolling and folding twice more.
  5. Roll the dough into a long rectangle and cut it into eight triangles.
  6. Roll each triangle up from the base, then place on a tray and proof at room temperature for 2 hours.
  7. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 200 C for 15 to 18 minutes, until deep golden.

Serving

Makes 8 croissants

Chef

Chef Harpreet Singh

Ingredients

  • Bread flour - 3 cups (375 g)
  • Sugar - 3 tbsp
  • Salt - 1.5 tsp
  • Instant yeast - 2 tsp
  • Milk - 0.75 cup
  • Water - 0.25 cup
  • Butter (for dough) - 2 tbsp, softened
  • Butter (for lamination) - 1 cup (225 g), cold

Team Size

2 members (including the chef)

Prep Time

2 days

Serving Guide

  • One croissant per guest
  • Serve warm with butter or jam
  • Warm for 3 minutes before serving

Customisation

  • Chocolate filling (pain au chocolat)
  • Almond topping
  • Savoury cheese or ham filling
