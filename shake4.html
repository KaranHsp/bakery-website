Classic Chocolate Milkshake

Item Name

Classic Chocolate Milkshake (rich chocolate ice cream shake with a chocolate sauce layer, topped with whipped cream, white chocolate shavings and a chocolate bar)

How It Is Made

  1. Blend the chocolate ice cream, milk and cocoa powder until thick and smooth.
  2. Spoon 1 tbsp of chocolate sauce into the bottom of each glass.
  3. Pour the milkshake over the sauce, letting a little sauce run down the inside of the glass.
  4. Pipe whipped cream on top in a swirl.
  5. Drizzle with the remaining chocolate sauce and sprinkle with white chocolate shavings.
  6. Push a chocolate bar into the cream at the side and add a straw.

Serving

Makes 5 shakes

Chef

Chef Harpreet Singh

Ingredients

  • Chocolate ice cream - 2 cups
  • Milk - 0.75 cup
  • Cocoa powder - 1 tbsp
  • Chocolate sauce - 3 tbsp, plus 1 tbsp for drizzle
  • Whipped cream - 0.75 cup
  • White chocolate shavings - 1 tbsp
  • Chocolate bar (finger size) - 2

Team Size

3 members (including the chef)

Prep Time

15 minutes

Serving Guide

  • One shake per guest
  • Serve in a tall footed glass
  • Serve immediately while thick

Customisation

  • Vanilla or strawberry ice cream base
  • Extra chocolate sauce on the sides
  • Malt powder for a richer flavour
