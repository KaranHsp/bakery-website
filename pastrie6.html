Cinnamon Roll

Item Name

Classic Cinnamon Roll (soft enriched dough rolled with brown sugar and cinnamon butter, finished with pearl sugar)

How It Is Made

  1. Warm the milk, dissolve the yeast and 1 tbsp of sugar in it, then leave for 5 minutes until foamy.
  2. Mix flour, remaining sugar and salt. Add the yeast mixture, egg and softened butter, then knead for 8 minutes until smooth.
  3. Cover and let the dough rise for 1 hour, until doubled in size.
  4. Roll the dough into a rectangle about 40 by 30 cm. Spread the soft butter over it.
  5. Mix brown sugar and cinnamon, then sprinkle evenly over the butter.
  6. Roll up tightly from the long edge, then cut into nine even slices.
  7. Place the slices in a greased tin, cover and rise for 45 minutes.
  8. Brush with beaten egg, scatter pearl sugar on top and bake at 190 C for 20 to 25 minutes, until golden.

Serving

Makes 9 rolls

Chef

Chef Harpreet Singh

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour - 3.5 cups
  • Milk, warm - 1 cup
  • Sugar - 0.25 cup, plus 1 tbsp for the yeast
  • Instant yeast - 2 tsp
  • Butter (for dough) - 0.25 cup, softened
  • Egg - 1, plus 1 for egg wash
  • Salt - 1 tsp
  • Butter (for filling) - 0.5 cup, softened
  • Brown sugar - 0.75 cup
  • Ground cinnamon - 2 tbsp
  • Pearl sugar - 2 tbsp

Team Size

3 members (including the chef)

Prep Time

4 hours

Serving Guide

  • One roll per guest
  • Serve slightly warm
  • Pairs well with coffee or tea

Customisation

  • Cream cheese icing
  • Raisins or chopped pecans in the filling
  • Cardamom in place of some cinnamon
