Almond Danish

Item Name

Almond Danish with Raspberry Jam (buttery puff pastry filled with almond frangipane, topped with raspberry jam and sliced almonds)

How It Is Made

  1. Beat butter and icing sugar until light, then add the egg and almond extract.
  2. Fold in the ground almonds and flour to make a smooth frangipane.
  3. Roll out the puff pastry and cut it into six squares.
  4. Fold the corners of each square into the centre, then press a border around the edges.
  5. Spoon frangipane into the centre of each pastry and top with a small spoon of raspberry jam.
  6. Brush the edges with beaten egg, scatter sliced almonds on top and chill for 15 minutes.
  7. Bake at 200 C for 18 to 22 minutes, until deep golden. Dust with icing sugar once cool.

Serving

Makes 6 turnovers

Chef

Chef Harpreet Singh

Ingredients

  • Puff pastry - 500 g (2 sheets)
  • Butter (for frangipane) - 0.5 cup
  • Icing sugar - 0.5 cup, plus 2 tbsp for dusting
  • Egg - 1, plus 1 for egg wash
  • Almond extract - 0.5 tsp
  • Ground almonds - 1 cup
  • Plain flour - 1 tbsp
  • Raspberry jam - 0.5 cup

Team Size

3 members (including the chef)

Prep Time

3 hours

Storage

  • Store in an airtight container at room temperature
  • Reheat for 5 minutes at 160 C to crisp the pastry
  • Best eaten on the day of delivery

Customisation

  • Apricot or cherry jam
  • Chocolate frangipane
  • Extra sliced almonds or icing sugar
