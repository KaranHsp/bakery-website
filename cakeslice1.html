Classic Vanilla Wedding Cake


Cake Name


Classic Vanilla Wedding Cake (3 tiers, white fondant with fresh flowers)


How It Is Made

  1. Bake three vanilla sponge layers in 6, 8 and 10 inch pans.
  2. Cool completely, then level the tops.
  3. Fill each layer with vanilla buttercream.
  4. Stack the tiers with dowels for support.
  5. Cover with smooth fondant and add the decorations.

Serving

Serves 100 guests

Chef

Chef Harpreet Singh

Team Size

5 members(including the chef)

Prep Time

2days

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour - 3 cups
  • Sugar - 2.5 cups
  • Butter - 1 cup
  • Eggs - 6
  • Milk - 1 cup
  • Vanilla extract - 2 tsp
  • Baking powder - 2 tsp
  • Fondant - 2 kg

Cutting Guide

  • 10 inch tier: about 50 servings
  • 8 inch tier: about 30 servings
  • 6 inch tier: about 20 servings
  • Total for 3 tiers: about 100 servings

Customisation

  • Custom names and dates on the top tier
  • Fresh or artificial flowers
  • Gold, silver or pastel colour themes
  • Eggless option available

Allergen Information

  • Contains: eggs, milk, wheat (gluten)
  • Made in a kitchen that also handles nuts
  • Please tell us about any allergies when ordering

Storage

  • Keep in a cool, dry place away from sunlight
  • Fridge storage is best if the room is hot
  • Best eaten within 2 days of delivery
