Red Velvet Cake

Cake Name

Red Velvet Cake with Cream Cheese Frosting (3 layers, topped with fresh strawberries and white chocolate dipped strawberries)

How It Is Made

  1. Whisk flour, cocoa, baking soda and salt together.
  2. Beat butter and sugar until light, then add eggs and vanilla.
  3. Mix in the red colouring, buttermilk and vinegar, then fold in the dry ingredients.
  4. Divide the batter into three 8 inch pans and bake until a toothpick comes out clean.
  5. Cool completely, then level the layers.
  6. Spread cream cheese frosting between and over the layers.
  7. Press the crumbs onto the sides and top with strawberries and white chocolate dipped strawberries.

Serving

Serves 20 guests

Chef

Chef Harpreet Singh

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour - 2.5 cups
  • Sugar - 1.5 cups
  • Butter - 0.5 cup
  • Eggs - 2
  • Buttermilk - 1 cup
  • Cocoa powder - 1 tbsp
  • Red food colouring - 2 tbsp
  • Vinegar - 1 tsp
  • Baking soda - 1 tsp
  • Vanilla extract - 2 tsp
  • Cream cheese - 500 g
  • Icing sugar - 2 cups
  • Strawberries - 8
  • White chocolate - 100 g

Team Size

3 members (including the chef)

Prep Time

1 day

Cutting Guide

  • Whole 8 inch cake: about 20 slices
  • Half cake: about 10 slices
  • Strawberry slices: one per guest

Customisation

  • Custom message piped on top
  • Extra strawberries or fresh flowers
  • Eggless option available
